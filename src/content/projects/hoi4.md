A research project on division design in Hearts of Iron IV patch 1.18. Three deliverables: a combat-model paper, a practical cookbook of templates by role, and an open-source simulator that exposes the model's sensitivities.

## Latest

Simulator walks the patch 1.18 combat resolution step by step. Paper drafted in Typst using the polymath-simple package. Cookbook mapping templates to roles (line, breakthrough, garrison, recon, marines, mountaineers) in progress.

## Status

Paper draft underway, simulator working, cookbook next.
