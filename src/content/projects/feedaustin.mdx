## What it is

A routing tool for Austin food rescue. Restaurants and grocers list surplus, pantries and shelters claim it, and the platform handles match and pickup timing.

## What I built

A scaffold of the matching service and a sketch of the partner intake flow. Paused while [[chalkiq]] and [[compiq]] take priority, but the problem still matters and the code is there.

## What I learned

Civic tech lives and dies by partner intake, not by software. Spending another week on the routing algorithm matters less than spending a week riding along with a pantry coordinator.
