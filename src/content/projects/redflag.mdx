## What it is

A consumer transparency platform. Search any brand and see where its money goes — political donations, lobbying, issue alignment — with every claim sourced. Web first, mobile to follow.

## What I built

- A brand-search backend that fans out to political donation, lobbying, and issue datasets.
- A React frontend with source attribution on every datapoint.
- A scaffold for upvote / downvote on companies, politicians, and influencers.

## What I learned

Transparency is a UI problem too. Raw FEC data is technically public but practically inaccessible. The work is not in the database query; it is in surfacing the right three numbers at the right depth so a user can act on them without going down a rabbit hole.
