## What it is

A comps tool for investors, agents, and home buyers. Enter an address, get a curated comp set, valuation, rent context, and a report-ready output without spreadsheet work.

## What I built

- A comps engine over RentCast data with FastAPI + asyncpg + Neon Postgres.
- A React 19 frontend with auth, subscription billing, and a buyer flow from address to report.
- A deploy pipeline split cleanly across Render (backend) and Vercel (frontend).

## What I learned

Comps are a UX problem dressed up as a data problem. The data exists; what doesn't exist is a workflow that respects the time of a buyer who has 15 minutes between showings. Every API call you cannot eliminate has to earn its place.
