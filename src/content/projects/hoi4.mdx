## What it is

A research project on division design in Hearts of Iron IV patch 1.18. Three deliverables: a combat-model paper, a practical cookbook of templates by role, and an open-source pipeline that simulates engagements and exposes the model's sensitivities.

## What I built

- A Python simulator that walks the patch 1.18 combat resolution step by step.
- A Typst paper laid out with the [polymath](https://github.com/dhayer200/polymath) package — proofs, definitions, and named templates as first-class blocks.
- A cookbook that maps templates to roles (line, breakthrough, garrison, recon, marines, mountaineers) with the math behind each choice.

## What I learned

Game balance is just an applied optimization problem with one weird constraint: the developers can change the objective function any time. Writing the paper changed how I read patch notes — every nerf is a perturbation of the same Lagrangian.
