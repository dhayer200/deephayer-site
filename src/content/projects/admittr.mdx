## What it is

A "chance me" that doesn't rely on Reddit. Students fill a profile, get per-college admission probability estimates blending a strength score with similarity-weighted historical outcomes, and see up to 20 comparable applicants with their actual results.

## What I built

- A similarity-weighted probability model over a curated applicant dataset.
- A swipe-based college discovery flow with admission outcome distributions.
- A passwordless auth flow (Resend magic links + JWT via jose).
- Separate transfer-student onboarding with GPA, credits, and transfer reasons.

## What I learned

Calibration eats accuracy for breakfast. A model that says "60%" needs to be right 60% of the time, not just on average. The hard part of admissions modeling is not the model — it is the schema design that makes longitudinal comparisons honest.
